## Video Guide: Aligning Your Mobile DJ Skills with the Perfect Event - PCDJ Techniques Revealed

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/mobiledjtips-matchtheevent.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/mobiledjtips-matchtheevent.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "mobiledjtips-matchtheevent")

**Sometimes the littlest detail can make the biggest impact — good or bad.**

In the video below Bill Hermann with [_The Entertainment Experience Workshops_](https://www.facebook.com/billcreates) discusses how much you, the entertainer and master of ceremonies (_not “Just the DJ”_) can impact the overall ambiance of the event your DJing.

When prepping for a gig be sure you know who your Mobile DJ client is, and how the event location will be prepped for the occasion. Yes, as a Pro DJ you should always be professional in your approach and your DJ rig should be a reflection of that, but if it’s a really high-end gig it’s best to pay even more attention to the small details. Don’t let your DJ equipment and presentation be the sticking point to an otherwise beautifully orchestrated event.
