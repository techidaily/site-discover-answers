## Explore the Voices of Rhythm: Premiere View of Iconic Singers on PCDJ DEX 3'S SideList

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/sidlist-singerslistcoverimage-1.jpg?resize=640%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/sidlist-singerslistcoverimage-1.jpg?fit=640%2C400&ssl=1 "sidlist-singerslistcoverimage (1)")

[**PCDJ DEX 3**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) will launch publicly soon with the goal to provide a single [**DJ mixing software**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) solution to mix everything – music, music videos, as well as host karaoke. 

_**(check out the last video preview of the DEX 3 browser [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/))**_

As outlined in [**this**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) previous DEX 3 blog post, the media file browser included with the (_FREE_) upgrade from DEX 2 has been a major focal point of the new platform; it’s been redesigned as a true database to be more robust (If you have 300,000 tracks in the library it will perform just as well as if you had 5!), responsive and cross-platform ready.  

**You now have three sections in the browser:** The browser/navigation section, the track list section and the new automix/sidelist pane – which as shown in the video below also doubles as a basic singers list for karaoke.

Album art, color coded tracks and flick gestures are just some of the improvements in DEX 3\. Any directory you browse via DEX 3 will _automatically_ be added to the internal database, and cached for quick searching. All browsed directories also display tag information so nesting directories as “_favorites_” in DEX 3 will likely be the most popular method for setting up the browser.
